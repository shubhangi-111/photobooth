# 📸 Pixel Booth

A browser-based digital photobooth for capturing moments, experimenting with filters, and creating downloadable photo layouts — all in one cute, minimal interface.

## ✨ Features

- **Live camera preview** — access your webcam directly in the browser.
- **Photo filters** — experiment with Original, Black & White, Vintage, Warm, Cool, and Y2K color effects.
- **Countdown capture** — enjoy a 3-second countdown before each photo.
- **Automatic photo sessions** — capture 1, 3, or 4 photos in one session.
- **Multiple layouts** — arrange your photos in a classic photo strip, a 2 × 2 grid, or a Polaroid-style composition.
- **Camera controls** — switch cameras where supported and toggle mirrored preview mode.
- **Flash effect** — simulate a camera flash during capture.
- **Retake photos** — start another session whenever you want.
- **PNG export** — download your finished photo composition directly to your device.

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Browser MediaDevices API
- HTML Canvas API

No backend, database, or account registration is required.

## 📷 How to Use

1. Open Pixel Booth and allow camera access.
2. Choose your preferred color filter.
3. Select a layout.
4. Choose the number of photos for your session.
5. Click the shutter button.
6. Pose during the countdown and let the booth capture your photos.
7. Review the generated composition.
8. Retake your photos or download the finished PNG.

## 🎨 Layouts

| Layout | Description |
|---|---|
| Photo Strip | A vertical arrangement of captured photos with a decorative footer. |
| 2 × 2 Grid | A square collage of up to four photos. |
| Polaroid | A single featured photo with a caption and date. |

## 🔒 Privacy

Pixel Booth uses the browser's camera API to capture photos. The current implementation processes images locally in the browser and does not require a backend or upload photos to a server.

Camera access is subject to browser permissions. Close the page when you finish using the booth to release the camera.

## 🧩 Current Scope

Pixel Booth is a functional prototype focused on the core photobooth experience.

Potential future enhancements include:

- AR face filters such as dog ears, cat whiskers, and crowns.
- Decorative stickers, sparkles, and doodles.
- Custom captions and themed photo frames.
